The product connects environmentally conscious and budget-friendly individuals with restaurants offering surplus end-of-day food at discounted prices through a mobile application

Discover a world where reducing food waste and saving money go hand-in-hand, all from the palm of your hand.

Key Features

  • ✓ Easy Browsing: Users can browse available food items by category, price, and location
  • ★ Secure Payment Processing: Users can pay for their orders securely through the app
  • → Real-time Updates: Restaurants can update food availability in real-time, ensuring users get the most up-to-date information
  • ⭐ Anonymous Browsing: Users can browse food items without knowing the restaurant or brand name
  • 📦 Order Tracking: Users can track the status of their orders from placement to delivery
