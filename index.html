The application connects budget-conscious foodies and environmentally aware consumers with restaurants offering surplus end-of-day food at discounted prices, promoting food waste reduction and affordable dining

Discover a world of affordable and sustainable dining, while making a positive impact on the environment.

Key Features

  • ✓ Anonymous Browsing: Browse available surplus food without knowing the restaurant or brand name
  • ★ Real-time Updates: Receive real-time notifications on order status and get updates from restaurants
  • → In-app Payment: Place and pay for orders entirely through the application
  • 🔍 Food Discovery: Search and filter function to find specific types of surplus food
  • 📊 Restaurant Dashboard: User-friendly interface for restaurants to manage surplus food listings and track orders
