A mobile application connecting customers with restaurants offering surplus end-of-day food at discounted prices, reducing food waste and supporting sustainable consumption

Discover a smarter way to enjoy your favorite food while making a positive impact on the environment.

Key Features

  • ✓ Anonymous Browsing: Allow customers to browse available food items without seeing restaurant or brand names
  • ★ Real-time Updates: Enable restaurants to update availability of surplus food items in real-time
  • → Secure Payment Processing: Integrate a secure payment system for customers to place orders and pay entirely through the app
  • ✓ Order Tracking: Provide customers with real-time updates on the status of their orders, from placement to delivery
  • 📊 Restaurant Dashboard: Create a dashboard for restaurants to manage their surplus food items, track orders, and analyze sales performance
