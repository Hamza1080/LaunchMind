A mobile application connecting customers with restaurants offering surplus end-of-day food at discounted prices

Discover a world of affordable, high-quality food while reducing food waste, one meal at a time.

Key Features

  • ✓ Anonymous Browsing: Customers can browse available food items without knowing the restaurant or brand name
  • ★ Real-time Updates: Restaurants can update the availability of their surplus food items in real-time
  • → In-app Payment: Customers can place and pay for orders entirely through the app
  • ✍️ Surplus Food Listing: Restaurants can list their leftover items and set discounted rates
  • 📦 Order Tracking: Customers can track the status of their orders from placement to delivery
