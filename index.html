A mobile app that helps freelancers track and manage unpaid invoices, ensuring timely payments and reducing financial stress

Take control of your finances and get paid on time with our intuitive and powerful invoice management tool.

Features

  • ✓ Invoice Tracking: A dashboard to view and manage all outstanding invoices, with filters and sorting options
  • ★ Payment Reminders: Automated reminders sent to clients when invoices are due or overdue, with customizable notification settings
  • → Client Management: A contact list to store client information, with notes and communication history
  • 📝 Invoice Generation: A template-based invoice generator, with auto-fill options and customizable templates
  • 📊 Reporting and Analytics: A dashboard to view invoice and payment trends, with insights on client behavior and revenue projections
