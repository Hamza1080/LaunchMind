A mobile application connecting customers with restaurants to purchase surplus end-of-day food at discounted prices, reducing food waste and providing affordable meal options

Discover a new way to enjoy your favorite foods while making a positive impact on the environment and your community.

Key Features

  • ✓ Anonymous Browsing: Browse available food items without knowing the restaurant or brand name
  • ★ Real-time Inventory Updates: Restaurants can update the availability of their surplus food items in real-time
  • → Secure Payment Processing: Secure and seamless payment process for customers to purchase food items through the app
  • ✍️ Order Management: Customers can track the status of their orders and receive notifications when their food is ready for delivery
  • 📊 Restaurant Dashboard: Restaurants can manage their surplus food items, update inventory, and track orders
